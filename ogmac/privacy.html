Privacy policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy describes Alex Genevsky’s personal ogmac calendar synchronization setup. It covers the local app and this information website, rather than other deployments of the open-source project.

Calendar information accessed

The app reads the selected university Exchange calendar through Apple Calendar on the Mac. It copies meeting titles, start and end times, locations, descriptions, availability and all-day information to a dedicated Google calendar. It does not copy the separate attendee list, although names or contact information already present in a meeting description may be included in that description.

On Google Calendar, the app reads event records and synchronization identifiers to find its previously created copies, then creates, updates or deletes those copies as needed. The Google authorization scope, calendar.events, permits event access across the account’s calendars; the app’s configuration limits its normal synchronization to the selected dedicated calendar.

How information is used

Google Calendar information is used to reconcile the app’s copies of source meetings. It is not used for advertising, profiling, selling data or training general-purpose AI models by the synchronization app.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The synchronization program runs locally on the owner’s Mac. OAuth tokens are stored in macOS Keychain. OAuth client configuration is stored in a local configuration file. Local synchronization state and diagnostic logs contain operational metadata, such as identifiers, timestamps and error information. Diagnostic logs can contain authorization-related information and should not be published or shared without review and redaction.

Meeting copies are stored in the selected Google calendar and are subject to the owner’s Google Calendar sharing settings. No meeting contents or authorization tokens are intentionally published on this website.

Sharing and service providers

The app sends copied meeting information directly to Google Calendar using Google’s API. It uses Apple’s local Calendar services to read the Exchange calendar. Microsoft, Apple and Google process information under their respective service terms and privacy policies. This setup does not use a separate calendar synchronization cloud intermediary and does not sell calendar information.

The owner may inspect local diagnostic information to operate or troubleshoot the setup. If the owner elects to share diagnostics with a support provider, the owner controls that disclosure and should remove credentials and unnecessary personal information first.

Retention and deletion

Copied events remain in Google Calendar until the app removes them during reconciliation or the owner deletes them. Events outside the configured synchronization window may remain. Stopping the program or revoking authorization does not automatically delete existing copies.

The owner can remove the dedicated destination calendar or its copied events through Google Calendar, and delete local configuration, state, logs and Keychain credentials on the Mac. Retention by Microsoft, Apple, Google or GitHub is governed by their respective services.

Stopping access

The owner can pause or disable the local synchronization job, revoke the app’s Google authorization through Google Account connections, or remove its Calendar access in macOS System Settings. Revoking access prevents future authorized synchronization but leaves existing copies in place until they are deleted separately.

This website

These app-information pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, account registration or calendar-upload forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This policy does not describe unrelated applications hosted elsewhere on the same website.
