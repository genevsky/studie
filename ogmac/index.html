Personal calendar utility

Work meetings in one place.

ogmac copies meetings from Alex Genevsky’s selected university Exchange calendar in Apple Calendar to a dedicated calendar in his personal Google account.

One-way synchronization on a Mac. The setup runs locally and periodically creates, updates or deletes its own Google Calendar copies to reflect the source calendar. Changes made to Google copies are not sent back to Exchange.

How this setup works

  • Apple Calendar provides the source meetings through macOS Calendar access.
  • Google authorization permits the local app to access Google Calendar events.
  • A dedicated destination calendar receives meeting titles, times, locations, descriptions, availability and all-day information. Separate attendee lists are not copied.
  • Google authorization tokens are kept in macOS Keychain. Local state and logs help the app track synchronization.

Personal use

This OAuth application is operated by Alex Genevsky for his own calendar synchronization. It is not a public sign-up service. This website displays information about the app; it does not receive calendar events or Google authorization tokens.

Privacy and control

Google’s event permission covers all calendars available to the signed-in account. This setup is configured to work with one dedicated destination calendar. The privacy policy explains access, storage, sharing, retention and revocation.

Copying university calendar information is subject to the university’s applicable policies. Meeting details and authorization credentials must not be uploaded to this public website.

Project and contact

The underlying software is the open-source ogmac project. This particular setup includes a local macOS permission helper and a credential-update adjustment for background operation.
